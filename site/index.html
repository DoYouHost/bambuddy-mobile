Bambuddy mobile

An Android & Wear OS companion for your self-hosted bambuddy server — monitor and control your Bambu Lab 3D printers from your phone or watch.

View source on GitHub Privacy policy
Requires your own bambuddy server. Bambuddy mobile is a companion app — it talks only to the self-hosted bambuddy server you point it at. It does not connect to Bambu Lab cloud on its own and will not work without a server. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Bambu Lab.

What it does

📡

Monitor prints

Live status, progress, layer, ETA and temperatures. A persistent notification keeps you posted, with configurable alerts for print events and hardware (HMS) errors.

🎛️

Control printers

Pause, resume and stop prints. Clear the plate, start the next job and reorder or manage the print queue.

🧵

Filament & hardware

Track your spool inventory and assign spools to AMS slots. Scan spool QR codes with the camera, control smart plugs and follow maintenance reminders.

📊

Archive & stats

Browse your print archive and statistics, and get an overview of projects, plates and parts.

🏠

Home-screen widget

See printer status at a glance and jump straight into the spool scanner from your home screen.

⌚

Wear OS companion

Check status and control prints right from your watch, paired with the phone app.

Privacy & open source

Bambuddy mobile has no analytics, no advertising and no cloud service. Your credentials are stored in encrypted, Keystore-backed storage on your device and are only ever sent to your own server. Read the full privacy policy.
